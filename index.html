Automation & AI for small business

Looking for help with AI? Start here.

A lot of owners come to me because someone told them they should be using AI. Maybe a vendor pitched it, or a competitor seems to be ahead. That is why I am here.

I build with AI tools every day, and sometimes they are the right answer. Just as often the better fix is simpler. A setting in software you already own. A service that costs forty dollars a month. A change in who does what.

My job is to tell you which is which, and to back it up with numbers from your own business. You get the fix that pays off, whatever it's called.

Questions

Common questions

Is Ledgeway an AI consulting firm?

Partly. I help small businesses decide where AI and automation make sense and then put them to work. But the goal is the right fix, and sometimes that's a process change or an off-the-shelf service. I'll recommend whatever pays off for your business.

Will I need to keep paying you after the 90 days?

No. Everything is built to be handed over. Your team gets training and written notes so they can run it and change it without me. If you want help with the year-one plan, that's a separate choice.

What does it cost?

The first engagement is a fixed fee agreed before any work starts. It depends on the size of the business and how much we build. We'll talk numbers on the intro call.

How much of my team's time will this take?

A short weekly check-in with your sponsor, plus a few hours with the people who do the work during the first month. After that it's mostly testing and training.

Do you only work in certain industries?

No. Trades, service businesses, and professional practices are where I see the most repeat work. Any business with 5 to 30 people and a lot of manual steps is a good fit.

Do you work on site?

Yes, across greater Boston, MetroWest, and Rhode Island. The first month usually includes time on site. The rest can be remote.

Tell me what eats your week.

Send a few lines about your business and the task you'd most like to stop doing. I'll reply within one business day to set up a 30-minute call. No pitch deck.
